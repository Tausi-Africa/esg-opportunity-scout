NOW  THEREFORE  THE  PARTNERS,  AFROPAVO, BSM  WASHAURI and  DENTONS
## AGREE TO COLLABORATE AS FOLLOWS:

## 1. PURPOSE & AGREEMENT
I- “Anchor Partners” shall mean Afropavo Analytics Limited, BSM
Washauri (TZ) Ltd, and Dentons EALC, who are the primary
signatories to this MoU and jointly responsible for the strategic
direction, governance, and execution of the partnership.

II- “Institutional Partners” shall mean organizations that collaborate with
the Anchor Partners in defined areas of implementation, knowledge
infrastructure, or capacity building, but who are not signatories to this
MoU unless otherwise agreed in writing.

III- The purpose of this strategic partnership agreement (“the
Agreement”) is to establish a legally binding framework for
cooperation between the Anchor Parties to jointly pursue, design, and
deliver projects, services, and solutions in agreed areas of
collaboration

IV- This Agreement defines the rights, obligations, and responsibilities of
the Anchor Partners and governs the strategic partnership.

## 2.  AREAS OF COLLABORATION.
Areas of Collaboration
The Parties agree to collaborate in the following key areas:
i. Green & Climate Financing: Parties shall jointly work together to support financial
institutions, corporates, and development partners to access green and climate
finance through:
a. Identification and structuring of green and climate finance opportunities;
b. Joint  development  and  submission  of  funding  proposals  aligned  with
international frameworks, standards, and donor requirements;

## 4 | Page

c. Advisory   services   on   environmental,   social,   and   governance   (ESG)
compliance;
d. Implementation,  and  management  of  Monitoring,  Evaluation  &  Learning
(MEL) systems;
e. Joint    reporting    and    communication    of    results    using    recognized
international standards.
ii. Structured  market  research  (Initial  Single-Industry  Focus): Parties  shall  jointly
identify  one  priority  industry  as  the  initial  focus  of  collaboration.  Within  the
selected industry, the Parties shall:
a. Develop standardized, data-driven research methodologies;
b. Conduct market, policy, and investment research for local and international
market entrants;
c. Produce  market  intelligence  outputs  to  support  investment  and  policy
decisions;
d. Jointly  disseminate  findings  through  reports,  briefs,  and  stakeholder
engagements.
iii. Knowledge  Hubs  &  Corporate  Training  Systems: Parties  shall  collaborate  to
establish sustainable training and knowledge systems, including:
a. Development of shared or interoperable knowledge hubs;
b. Creation of post-training follow-up mechanisms to ensure long-term
impact;
c. Joint development of corporate training curricula, frameworks, and
toolkits;
d. Continuous maintenance and updating of shared knowledge repositories.
The  Parties  acknowledge  that  delivery  of  Green  &  Climate  Finance  initiatives  requires
integrated technical, legal, institutional, and knowledge-based capabilities. Accordingly,
the  collaboration  shall  be  implemented  through coordinated  roles  undertaken  by  the
Anchor Partners, with support from identified Institutional Partners where relevant.

## 3. RESPONSIBILITIES OF THE PARTIES
A. BSM Washauri Shall:
i. Provide strategic advisory, governance, policy, and institutional development
expertise;
ii. Lead or co-lead stakeholder engagement, communications, and client
relationship management;
iii. Contribute to the design of governance frameworks and MEL systems;
iv. Lead or co-lead business development, proposal structuring, and contracting;
v. Provide qualified experts, trainers, and facilitators for partnership activities.

B. AFROPAVO Shall:
i. Provide technical expertise in climate finance, green financing instruments,
and applied research;
ii. Lead or co-lead technical analysis, data collection, and market research
execution;
iii. Support development of funding proposals, technical documentation, and
impact metrics;
iv. Provide technical inputs to training materials, knowledge hubs, and learning
platforms;
v. Support innovation, applied research, and technical quality assurance.
C. DENTONS Shall:
vi. Provide legal, regulatory, and fiduciary advisory support in relation to Green
and Climate Finance initiatives;
vii. Advise on ESG compliance frameworks, international climate finance
standards, and donor-aligned governance requirements, including those
applicable to Green Climate Fund (GCF)–related transactions;
viii. Support structuring and review of climate and green finance instruments,
funding agreements, and associated legal documentation;
ix. Provide legal risk assessment and mitigation advisory across the project
lifecycle;

x. Support development and review of governance frameworks to ensure
institutional credibility and regulatory compliance.

- INSTITUTIONAL PARTNERS (CEOrt)
The Anchor Partners may collaborate with selected Institutional Partners to support
implementation of specific components of the partnership.
Without prejudice to the foregoing, CEOrt Tanzania is recognized as an Institutional
Partner to this MoU and shall:
 Provide access to its established digital knowledge hub and learning platforms
for purposes of ESG, sustainability, and climate finance capacity building;

 Support delivery of structured training, post-engagement learning, and
continuous knowledge dissemination;

 Collaborate with the Anchor Partners to ensure sustainability of capacity-
building interventions without duplication of platforms or systems.
Institutional Partners shall not be considered parties to this MoU and shall not assume
rights or obligations hereunder unless expressly agreed in writing through a separate
agreement.
## 5- DURATION
This MoU is valid for a period of one (3) year commencing on the last date of signing
by the signatories of the party’s representatives. This MoU may not be amended or
modified except with the written consent of both parties. Neither party may assign or
subcontract its rights or obligations under this MoU in whole or in part without the
prior written of the other party.
## 6- COMPENSATION AND PAYMENT TERMS

## 9- TERMINATION
9.1This MOU may be terminated in any of the following circumstances:
i. Immediately upon the insolvency of or filing a petition of bankruptcy by either
Party or any other person.
ii. Upon written notice by the aggrieved Party in the event of a breach of any
term or obligation herein that has gone uncured for a period of fourteen (14)
days after notice of such breach has been given to the defaulting Party.
iii. Without cause upon thirty (30) days’ prior written notice by either Party.
iv. Upon mutual Consent of the Parties.
v. Upon reaching its full term of 1 year unless renewed.
Expiration or termination of this MOU shall be without prejudice to all accrued rights
and liabilities of either Party nor shall it affect the coming into force or continuance
in force of any provision hereof which is expressly or by implication intended to come
into or continue in force on or after such termination.

## 10- GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTE RESOLUTION
The governing law and language for this MoU will be the laws of the United Republic
of  Tanzania.  Any  controversy  or  claim  arising  out  of  or  relating  to  this  MoU,  or  any
breach thereof (hereinafter, a "Dispute”) that cannot be resolved between the parties
within  fourteen  (14)  days  will  be  submitted  for  arbitration  in  accordance  with  the
Arbitration Act, Act No. 2 of 2020. The place of arbitration shall be Dar es Salaam and
the language to be used in the arbitral proceedings shall be English.

## 11- LIABILITY BETWEEN THE PARTIES
No party shall be liable to the other(s) for damages of any kind or nature because of
this MoU or the rendering of the services.

## 13- NO JOINT VENTURE OR PARTNERSHIP
Nothing contained in this MoU will be construed as creating any agency, partnership,
joint  enterprise  or  other  similar  relationship  between  the  parties.  The  relationship
between the parties will always be that of independent contractors. Neither party will
have authority to contract for or bind the other in any manner whatsoever.  This MoU
confers no rights upon either party except those expressly granted herein and does
not confer any right upon either party to make any representation or commitment on
behalf of the other.
